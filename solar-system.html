Explore the planets and moons in 3D

Travel from Mercury to Neptune, follow the moons around their parent planets, and view Saturn’s rings edge-on or from above. Search for a body like Europa, Titan, or Ceres to find it directly.

Open the 3D explorer

Compare the Solar System, then look closer

Choose Overview to see the planets together. This view enlarges the planets and compresses the distances between them. Select a body to switch to Exploration, where the scene uses physical scale and you can drag to orbit the target.

Read the imagery and position labels

Open an object’s information panel to see its imagery sources and coverage limits. The explorer combines historical spacecraft and telescope mosaics with modeled lighting. The position label distinguishes objects with JPL-based positions from objects on simplified orbits.
