Explore the Apollo 17 landing region

Descend into the Taurus–Littrow valley, then look across the lunar hills and walk on terrain reconstructed from NASA Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter measurements and imagery.

Open the 3D explorer

From lunar orbit to the valley

  1. Open Search and choose Moon.
  2. Choose Descend to surface on the Taurus–Littrow card.
  3. Drag to look around. Use WASD or the on-screen arrows to walk. Choose Return to orbit to leave the surface.

What you see on the surface

The valley’s broad terrain and registered imagery come from orbital observations. Close ground grain and scattered stones illustrate details smaller than the source data can resolve. The surface panel shows the modeled Sun angle, elevation, and position.
