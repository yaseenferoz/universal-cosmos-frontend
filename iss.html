  1. Open Search and select ISS, or choose Visit ISS from Earth.

  3. Use Earth observation to pause, change speed, scrub the orbit, or jump to a modeled daylight pass near a region.

What the simulation represents

The station follows a modeled circular orbit at 400 km. Its ground track shifts as Earth rotates beneath it, and sunlight changes as the station goes around the planet. Earth’s surface comes from historical NASA imagery. The station view uses a simulated position and historical models.
