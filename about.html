Universal Cosmos

About Universal Cosmos

Universal Cosmos is a 3D Solar System explorer that runs in your browser. Fly around planets, descend into the Apollo 17 landing region, or watch Earth pass beneath the International Space Station.

How the explorer is built

The explorer uses React and Three.js. It combines historical spacecraft and telescope imagery with measured lunar terrain and modeled lighting. The object panels explain where the imagery comes from and what it covers.

The explorer calculates supported bodies’ positions from JPL data and uses simplified orbits for other paths. The ISS visit follows a simulated orbit and uses historical station models and Earth imagery.
